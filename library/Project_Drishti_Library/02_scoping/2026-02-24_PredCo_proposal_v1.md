To: Lokesh Jain (Procurement), Arvind Rao (Head EHS), Ardent Cement Works Ltd
From: PredCo (Panscience Predictive Technologies Pvt Ltd) | Ref: PC/PROP/2026/019 v1 | 24 Feb 2026
Response to RFQ ACWL-BHD-EHS-2026-07

## 1. Understanding

Bhadrapur records PPE compliance through two announced safety walks a day. 146 existing cameras record but are not watched
outside the gate and stores. Four lost-time injuries occurred in 2025, two in Q4. The unit is preparing for the group safety
audit in October 2026.

## 2. Proposed solution

ViewOS, PredCo's visual intelligence platform, runs on edge GPU servers inside the plant's OT network and reads the existing
camera streams. No new cameras are required for helmet, vest and restricted-zone detection. Alerts go to the control room
screen and to the area in-charge on WhatsApp through an on-premise gateway.
