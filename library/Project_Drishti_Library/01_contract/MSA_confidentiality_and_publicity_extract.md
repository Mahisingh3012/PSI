# Master Services Agreement: extract

Between Ardent Cement Works Limited ("Ardent" or "the Company") and Panscience Predictive Technologies Private Limited ("PredCo" or "the Vendor").
Agreement no. ACWL/LGL/MSA/2026/0112, effective 2 March 2026. Extract of clauses 14, 15 and Annexure D only.

## 14. Confidential Information

14.1 "Confidential Information" includes all information disclosed by the Company in any form, including plant layouts,
production capacities, process parameters, video footage, images of personnel or premises, safety incident records,
commercial terms, and the identity of the Company's sites and suppliers.

14.2 The Vendor shall use Confidential Information solely to perform the Services and shall not disclose it to any third
party without the Company's prior written consent.

14.3 Video footage and images captured under this Agreement remain the property of the Company and shall not leave the
Company's premises or network, except anonymised training samples approved in writing by the Head, IT and OT Security.

14.4 Obligations under this clause survive for five (5) years after termination.

## 15. Publicity and references

15.1 Neither party shall use the other's name, logo or trademarks in any publicity, marketing material, case study,
award entry or customer list without the other party's prior written approval, given by an authorised signatory.

15.2 Notwithstanding 15.1, the Vendor may describe the Services in anonymised form, provided that the description:
(a) identifies the Company only as "an Indian cement manufacturer" or equivalent industry description;
(b) does not disclose any location below the level of country;
(c) does not disclose production capacity, number of plants, or headcount of the Company;
(d) does not include images of the Company's premises, personnel, vehicles or signage; and
(e) is shared with the Company for review at least ten (10) business days before first use.

15.3 Approval under 15.1 or review under 15.2(e) shall be sought from the Company's Corporate Communications
department with a copy to Legal. Approval by plant personnel alone shall not constitute approval.

15.4 Any approval may be withdrawn by written notice, after which the Vendor shall stop new use within thirty (30) days.

## Annexure D: Data handling (summary)

- All processing on premises, on Vendor-supplied edge servers inside the Company's OT network.
- No internet egress from edge servers. Vendor remote access only through the Company's jump host with session recording.
- Video retained for 30 days; alert snapshots for 180 days; no facial recognition or biometric processing unless separately
  approved under the Company's privacy policy and applicable law.
- Model retraining on site images permitted on premises only; images to be blurred for faces before any export.
