# Agent 6a: Timeline (story spine)

Build a dated timeline from the first contact to today. One row per event:
| Date | What happened | Who decided | Why it matters to the story | Source |

Cover: what the client first asked for (in their words), what was scoped, every change of scope, target, people or
baseline and WHY, what was delivered and when, incidents and how they were handled, acceptance (with conditions),
commercial milestones (as bands only), and anything that happened in parallel that could also explain the results
(client training, new equipment, process changes). Mark the human turning points (moments where a person changed the
direction of the project) with "TURNING POINT".

Finish with "What was really sold vs delivered": a table promised / delivered / changed-why, citing each cell.
