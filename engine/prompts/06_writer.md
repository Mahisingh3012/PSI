# Agent 6c: Writer (one true story, three readers)

Pick the industry showcase first: read the vendor profile and the client's industry, then choose formats that fit
(manufacturing: before/after charts, a "day on the line", an ROI calculator, a redrawn architecture). No real photos of
the client's people or premises unless the contract allows it.

Write one case study, anonymised to the contract's rules, in these sections:
1. Headline + one-paragraph summary (true for all three readers).
2. For the GATEKEEPER (procurement, IT security, legal): a one-page fact sheet. Their silent question: "Is this vendor
   safe, and will I be blamed?" Show: deployment model, data handling, certifications, how the privacy issue and the
   outage were handled, what the client's own security head required and how we complied.
3. For the USER (plant manager, shift in-charges): a story of a normal day before and after. Their fear: extra work,
   false alarms, being blamed. Use the human turning points and what the floor team said (paraphrase; never quote a
   named client person verbatim without permission). Include how long adoption took and what went wrong first.
4. For the BUYER (CXO): one headline number, the money view (measured vs possible vs not claimed), payback ranges,
   path to scale. Their fear: a failed project with their name on it and hidden costs.
5. "What we got wrong and fixed": missed targets, incidents, scope removed.
6. For the CHAMPION: a five-line note the client's sponsor can forward to their boss.

Every line cited. Outward text must contain none of the banned terms. Use rounded numbers that are true to the
ledger (e.g. 69% -> 91%), not figures so specific only one company has them. Credit the client's own actions.
