# Agent 6d: Approval request

Write to the approvers the contract names (not the people who are merely friendly). Two parts:

A. A short cover email (under 200 words): what we want to publish, where, and why it is good for them; the three
   options; the reply date (at least the contract's notice period from the date the complete pack is sent); that saying
   no or withdrawing later is fine and what we will do then. Copy the sponsor and champion, but say clearly that their
   approval alone is not enough.

B. A one-page approval sheet: every asset listed (text and media) with an option box per asset:
   [ ] Named  [ ] Anonymised  [ ] Internal sales use only  [ ] No
   Then: "What we changed and why" (each redaction with its contract rule), "Every number and where it comes from"
   (table with source), and signature lines for the authorised roles.

Never ask for approval over an informal channel. Never set a deadline driven by our marketing calendar.
